A MORE PERSONAL WAY TO LAYER

About OLFACULATOR

Why subjective?

Your nose is the source of truth. Published note pyramids can be incomplete, inconsistent, or different from what you actually smell. You do not need to identify every material by name. Choose the notes and impressions that best describe your experience.

Layering tips

  • Layer simple + complex. Let a simpler fragrance support a more intricate one without making the blend feel too busy.
  • Connect familiar notes. Layer notes from the same fragrance family or build around accords that are already known to work well together.
  • Respect projection. Avoid pairing very soft + very loud fragrances without testing first, because the louder scent can overpower the softer one. Adjust spray counts or choose closer projection levels.
