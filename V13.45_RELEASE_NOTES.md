# RIVALSNOTEBOOK v13.45 Release Notes

## Counterpicker

- Matchup target identity now appears on the right as `VS + hero portrait + hero name`.
- Counter hero summary order is now counter tier, total points, then HIDE on the right.
- The matchup description sits below the target identity. Source attribution is left-aligned and EDIT is right-aligned beneath the description.
- Matchup vote, comment count and contribution points remain in the footer.

## Synergies

- Teammate identity remains right-aligned as `WITH + hero portrait + hero name`.
- Source attribution and EDIT share the row directly beneath the description, with EDIT on the right.
- Contribution points remain at the far right of the vote/comment footer rather than moving into the teammate identity row.
- Suggested-hero total points and HIDE remain aligned at the right of the Synergies card header.
- No Group Synergy Rules icons or behaviors were removed.

The changes are layout-only and preserve existing vote, comment, edit, group-rule and Playbook behavior.
